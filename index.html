About Medhaan

One governance standard across every line we run.

Medhaan is a multi-sector holding company focused on execution-led delivery. We build and operate business lines under a single governance umbrella and execute through group entities, partners and project-specific SPVs.

Our work supports government, PSU and enterprise clients who need reliable delivery, clean documentation and clear accountability from planning through handover.

  • Multi-sector business lines under one governance umbrella
  • Delivery via group entities, partners and ring-fenced SPVs
  • Safety-first, documentation-led execution with clear escalation
Business Lines
